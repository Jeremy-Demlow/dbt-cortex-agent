# Governance

`dbt_cortex_agent` is maintainer-led. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions;
incorporation does not transfer ownership. Contributions are initially coordinated privately.

Merge, release, governance-policy, security-advisory, maintainer-appointment, and publication
decisions require approval by two distinct maintainers. [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) records
one maintainer and a vacancy, so those actions are currently blocked.

Before public publication, the release record must contain two-maintainer approval, documented
contributor and employer authorization, legal review of licensing/privacy/export/trademark and
non-affiliation language, and a completed [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md)
review with no unresolved terms. Missing evidence blocks publication; no review is implied.
